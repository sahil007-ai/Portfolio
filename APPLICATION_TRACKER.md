## Volume & rhythm

Realistic minimum to get any traction:

- **Week 1–2:** 30 applications (3-5/evening). All Tier A + half of Tier B.
- **Week 3–4:** 20 applications. Remaining Tier B + all Tier C + new listings from aggregators.
- **Week 5+:** 10/week from new aggregator listings.

**Total over 6 weeks: 70–80 applications.** Industry response rate for student GenAI roles in India is roughly 5–15%. So that's **~5–12 first-round interviews**, of which ~30% should convert. **Mathematical expected outcome: 1–4 offers.**

The math says 30 applications is not enough. 70+ is.

## Application discipline (read every Sunday)

1. **Did I apply to >= 5 this week?** If no, identify the blocker.
2. **Did I keep building?** Even one commit per day to a project counts.
3. **Did I post on LinkedIn / X this week?** Even 1 post = visibility.
4. **Did I cold-DM 1 practitioner?** (See LEARNING_ROADMAP.md → Outlier move #3.)
5. **Did I update this tracker?** A tracker you don't update is a TODO list with shame attached.

The rejection emails will hurt the first three weeks. By week 4 they stop hurting because volume mutes them.

The ones that convert tend to come from places you almost didn't apply to. Cast wide. Don't pre-reject yourself.
